## The one simplification

"Updates that don't change the indexed column **don't** require an index update."

The rubric's own phrasing is softer — "**may** avoid index maintenance" — and the softer version is
the accurate one. In Postgres this is the HOT optimisation, and it only applies when the new tuple
fits on the same page; a non-HOT update writes a new tuple version and **every** index has to point
at it, even indexes on untouched columns.

Not a deduction. It is the standard simplification, it's directionally right, it holds in the
common case, and the rubric lists the whole point as optional. Recording it because a stricter
grader might see "don't" where the key says "may".
