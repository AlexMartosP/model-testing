## Why 2

**`sudo` is never mentioned, and `2>/dev/null` hides the reason it matters.**

Checked on this machine: running its command without sudo produces **4 `Permission denied`
errors** under `/var/log/jumpcloud*`. Those files are silently excluded from the ranking. If one of
them were large, the answer to "the 10 largest files" would simply be wrong, and nothing in the
output would say so.

The key asks for "mentions that sudo may be needed" as one of its three bullets, and this is the
case where redirecting stderr is worse than omitting the note: `2>/dev/null` converts a visible
"you need root" signal into a clean-looking but incomplete list.

**Its own verification couldn't have caught this.** The single `bash` call it ran was:

```
du -ah /var/log 2>/dev/null | sort -rh | head -10
```

stderr suppressed. So "(verified working)" is true but shallow: it confirmed the pipeline runs, not
that the result is complete. Hiding the errors is exactly what prevented its check from surfacing
the gap.
