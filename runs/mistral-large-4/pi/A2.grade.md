## Why 2 and not 3

Two deductions, both for false precision rather than fabrication:

1. **DKIM described as a CNAME record.** It's a TXT record. CNAME belongs to link tracking. A
   reader following this would look for the wrong record type.
2. **The menu path is stated flatly and is not quite right.** "Settings → Sending domains" skips
   the Workspace Settings level. Hedged, this would have cost nothing; asserted as where to go in
   the reader's own account, it's the kind of detail that sends someone hunting.

The hedged parts cost nothing at all. "Typically DKIM/CNAME records" carries its hedge, and the
SPF and verification-flow claims are simply correct.
