## It explains *why* the premise fails

The key just says the secret may survive. The answer gives the mechanism, which is the part that
actually changes behaviour:

> GitHub caches commits and keeps them accessible by SHA for a while, even after force push

Correct, and the most common gap in people's mental model — a force-pushed commit becomes dangling
rather than deleted, and remains reachable by SHA until garbage collection that GitHub does not run
promptly. This is why step 4 (asking Support to purge) exists at all.
