Done. The "Quiet hours" panel now appears above the incident list whenever a home is selected.

**What was added**

- `web/src/components/QuietHours.tsx` — fetches *all* incidents for the selected home (not just open ones, since a resolved noise spike still coloured its hour) and renders 24 cells, oldest on the left.
- `web/src/format.ts` — pure, unit-tested helpers: `last24HourCells()` buckets incidents into the 24 whole hours ending at the current hour and keeps the worst kind per hour (noise > occupancy > offline > none), plus `formatHour()` and `summarizeHours()`.
- `web/src/App.tsx` — wraps the selected home's panels in a `.home-view` stack.
- `web/src/styles.css` — new severity colours as custom properties (`--noise` reuses `--alert`, plus `--occupancy`, `--offline`, `--quiet`); no hex values in components, per the conventions. No new dependencies.

**Mouse-free access** — each cell carries a full `aria-label` ("2 PM to 3 PM: noise"), a visible letter mark (N/O/D) so colour isn't the only signal, the strip is keyboard-focusable (`tabIndex={0}`) so it can be scrolled with arrow keys, and a plain-text summary ("Last 24 hours: 2 noisy hours, …") plus a legend spell out the meaning entirely in words.

**Phone readability** — below 48rem the layout already collapses to one column; the strip keeps its 24-cell oldest-on-left order and scrolls horizontally with cells at a readable 2.1rem minimum width instead of shrinking into illegibility.

Verified: `npm test` (12 passed, including new bucketing/severity/edge-case tests), `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, and a live curl against `be` confirming the incidents endpoint shape.
